The ₹50 Closed-Loop Ring-Oscillator TDR (Replaces ₹30,000 Timing Chips)

$$f = \frac{v}{4 \cdot d} \iff d = \frac{v}{4 \cdot f}$$

Why Jal Jeevan Outperforms Conventional Tech

1. 100% Pipe Material Agnostic

Acoustic leak loggers fail on HDPE/PVC plastic pipes because plastic dampens sound waves. Jal Jeevan senses external water contact directly, working on 100% of pipeline materials.

2. Meter Pinpoint vs Zone Guess

Flow meters only tell you a 10 km zone is losing water. Jal Jeevan pinpoints the leak down to the exact meter mark, saving digging miles of rural roads.

3. 95% Cost Reduction

Commercial TDR cables (TraceTek/TTK) are imported and cost ₹5,000–₹10,000/meter. Jal Jeevan uses a composite cable manufactured locally for under ₹30/meter.

4. ₹50 Ring-Oscillator Timing
