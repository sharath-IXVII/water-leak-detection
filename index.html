The ₹50 Inverter Ring-Oscillator (Replaces ₹30,000 Timing Instruments)

An ESP32 ADC is 50x too slow to sample picosecond pulses. A single 74LVC14 Schmitt trigger inverter (₹15) recirculates the reflection into a resonant delay loop. The cable delay becomes an oscillating frequency counted directly by the ESP32 hardware pulse counter (PCNT) up to 40 MHz:

f = v / (4 · d)  ⟺  d = v / (4 · f)

⚡ Power-Over-Trench Architecture (Zero Dead Batteries Underground)

Batteries buried underground eventually die and leave pipelines blind. Jal Jeevan runs an inexpensive 24V DC auxiliary power wire along the pipe trench from the Gateway's above-ground solar station. Each node has a rechargeable micro-UPS buffer ensuring continuous 24/7 monitoring even during municipal grid cuts!

Why Jal Jeevan Outperforms All 5 Conventional Alternatives

1. 100% Pipe Material Agnostic

Acoustic leak loggers fail on HDPE/PVC plastic pipes because plastic dampens sound waves. Jal Jeevan senses water directly, working on 100% of pipeline materials.

2. Exact Meter Pinpoint vs Zone Guess

Flow meters only tell you a 10 km zone is losing water. Jal Jeevan pinpoints the leak down to the exact meter mark (±1 m), saving digging miles of roads.

3. 98.6% Cost Reduction
